About

At the park next to my parents’ home where I used to play at when I was young, the ruins of ancient people who had lived there were excavated from under the ground. I tried to imagine the people who had lived there. That was probably the last period in human history when there was still no war.
Since then, I have been searching for answers to peace that lie on the border between nature and civilization.

1994 Tokyo Zokei University,BFA in Photography
